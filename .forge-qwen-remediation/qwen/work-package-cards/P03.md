## Required focused tests

- zip-slip rejected
- symlink escape rejected
- case/unicode collision rejected
- mutation during ingestion rejected or quarantined
- duplicate content deduplicated
- source deletion after ingestion does not affect stored package
- crash recovery at every ingestion state
