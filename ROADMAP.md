## Status and acceptance

**Recorded** means the linked PR is merged and supplies the stated narrow evidence. **Needs check** means implementation exists but this milestone's current evidence/repair is not closed. **Open** means an unfulfilled requirement. **Ready in PR** means proposed, not accepted. **Blocked** retains its exact missing dependency. **Deferred** requires an owner decision and never means complete.

Every direct update records its affected row with evidence and limitation. Historical PR links remain tied to their original revisions; GitHub's merge state determines acceptance only for that history. A phase also requires README, Unreleased changelog, affected-document closeout, and local/remote `main` plus Xcode synchronization. Do not mark a phase complete from a source update or successful build alone.
